Snoozing again? Meet Gloop, your wake-up buddy.

With Gloop, your alarm doesn't just switch off. To stop it, you do one small thing first: solve a quick maths problem, shake your phone, or scan your toothbrush in the bathroom. By the time you're done, you're awake. No more snooze spiral.

⏰ AN ALARM THAT DOESN'T GIVE UP
• Rings through silent mode and Focus, like a real alarm
• Press stop without doing your mission and it rings again a few minutes later
• A live countdown on your Lock Screen shows when it comes back
• A fail-safe stops it after a set number of rings, so you never get stuck

🧩 10 MISSIONS THAT WAKE YOU UP
• Brain: Maths problems in a row, a quiz on a topic you pick, memory match or a tongue twister
• Body: shake your phone until you're up
• Out of bed: scan a code you stuck in another room, scan your toothbrush or coffee mug, take a photo of the sky, show your made bed, or hunt for something in a random colour
• Camera off or item lost? A harder backup mission takes over, so there's always a way out

🟠 GLOOP KEEPS YOU HONEST
• Gloop bounces when you get up on the first ring
• Snooze and miss mornings, and he slowly droops into a sad little puddle
• Earn XP every morning and evolve Gloop from a tiny Droplet to King Gloop, with a new accessory at every stage
• Collect stickers for streaks, first-ring mornings and comebacks
• Choose how Gloop talks to you: gentle, tough love or savage

👯 WAKE UP WITH FRIENDS
• Create a club with friends, family or your gym, or join a public one
• See who's up, climb the weekly leaderboard, and share a morning selfie that disappears after 24 hours

🎵 YOUR SOUNDS
• Classic tones and melodies, plus your own sounds: import a file or record a message
• Wake up to a song from Apple Music while you do your mission (Apple Music subscription required)

📱 MORE
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets with your streak and Gloop
• Pause all alarms for a holiday
• Light and dark mode, and app icons in five Gloop colours
• Works without an account. Your alarms and scans stay on your iPhone

GLOOP PRO
Gloop is free with one alarm and the Maths mission. Gloop Pro unlocks unlimited alarms, every mission, all sounds, your own sounds, Apple Music and the larger widgets. Try it free for 3 days.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple ID settings. A lifetime option is also available.

Gloop is not a medical device. For important wake-ups, always use a second alarm too.
