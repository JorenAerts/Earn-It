# Community Guidelines

Effective {{effectiveDate}}

Buddies is about motivating each other to get up. These guidelines keep it friendly and safe. They apply to clubs, club names and descriptions, morning photos and reactions. Breaking them can get your content removed and your account blocked.

## Be kind

- Cheer each other on. Friendly teasing within your own club is fine; bullying, threats and harassment are not.
- No hate speech or discrimination based on who someone is, including origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or age.

## Morning faces only

- Only post photos of yourself. Don't post other people without their permission, and never children other than yourself.
- Nothing nude, sexual or suggestive. Nothing violent, gory, or showing self-harm, drugs or weapons.
- No private information in photos: documents, screens with personal data, your address or other people's.

## Respect privacy

- What's shared in a club stays in the club. Don't screenshot other members' photos or results to post elsewhere.
- Don't pretend to be someone else, and don't create accounts to get around a block.

## Clubs

- Public club names and descriptions must be friendly for everyone. No insults, sexual references or hate.
- Club owners: keep your club safe. You can remove members who break these rules.
- No spam, advertising or chain messages.

## Reporting and blocking

- **Report:** long-press a photo or a person and choose Report, or report a club from its Members page. Tell us briefly what's wrong.
- **Block:** long-press a person and choose Block. You won't see each other's photos, times or ranking anymore. Manage blocked people in Settings › Buddies.

## What we do

- We review reports within 24 hours.
- Depending on what happened, we remove content, remove a member from a club, delete a club, or suspend or delete an account. Serious cases (such as illegal content or threats) are removed immediately and may be reported to the authorities.
- Morning photos are deleted automatically after 24 hours anyway; reports are kept up to 12 months to handle repeat abuse.

## Appeals

If you think we got it wrong, email {{supportEmail}} and tell us what happened. We'll take another look.

## Need help?

If someone's message or photo worries you, or you feel unsafe, talk to someone you trust. In an emergency, call 112 (EU) or your local emergency number.
