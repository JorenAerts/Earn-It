## Data that stays on your iPhone

The following is stored only on your device and is never sent to us:

- Your alarms (times, days, labels, missions, sounds) and your wake-up history, streaks, stages and stickers.
- Your own alarm sounds: imported audio and recordings you make with the microphone.
- Item scans for the "Scan your item" mission: a cut-out picture of the item and a mathematical "feature print" used to recognise it. Registered codes for the "Scan a code" mission.
- Your settings: appearance, app icon, coach tone, reminder time, paused alarms.

The camera and microphone are only used for the feature you start (scanning, recording, the morning photo) and are processed on your iPhone. Reminders are local notifications created on your device.

If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. iOS may include it in your own iCloud or computer backup, which we have no access to.

## Account (optional)

You can use {{appName}} without an account. If you create one, we process:

- Your email address and how you sign in (email and password, or Apple, Google, Facebook or Microsoft).
- Your display name, your wake-up goal (time, days, what makes mornings hard) and your onboarding answers (age range, how you found us, how you react to alarms, how long until you get up, your goal).

Why: to give you an account, keep your progress and profile, and run Buddies. Legal basis: performance of our agreement with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The optional onboarding answers are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)); you can skip them.

If you sign in with Apple, Google, Facebook or Microsoft, that provider tells us your email address and confirms who you are. Their own privacy policies apply to what they process.

## Buddies (optional)

If you join or create a club, we process:

- The clubs you create or join, your role, and the club name, emoji and description.
- Your morning results: the day, when you finished your mission (you can hide the exact time in Settings › Buddies), the number of rings, XP, streak and stage.
- Morning photos you choose to post, and the clubs you share them with. **Photos are deleted after 24 hours.**
- Reactions you give, reports you send and people you block.

Other members of your clubs see your display name, your morning results and the photos you shared with that club. Public clubs can be found by anyone signed in; their members are not shown to non-members.

Legal basis: performance of our agreement with you (Art. 6(1)(b)). Reports and blocks are also processed to keep Buddies safe (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)).

## Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details. Apple tells the app which plan you have. If you are signed in, the purchase can carry your account ID so your Pro access is linked to your account. Apple's privacy policy applies to payments.

## Anonymous usage statistics

To understand which features work and where people get stuck, {{appName}} sends anonymous usage statistics to TelemetryDeck (Germany): for example which screens are opened, how onboarding and mornings go, and which plan is chosen. These statistics:

- use a random ID created on your iPhone, not linked to your account, which TelemetryDeck hashes again;
- never contain your name, email, alarm names, photos, club names or anything you type;
- include technical details such as app version, iOS version and device model.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving {{appName}} (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can switch this off at any time in Settings › Privacy; the random ID is then deleted.

## Who processes data for us

- **Supabase** (hosting of accounts, Buddies and photos; servers in the EU).
- **TelemetryDeck** (anonymous usage statistics; Germany).
- **Apple** (App Store, payments, Sign in with Apple, Apple Music).
- **Google, Facebook, Microsoft** (only if you choose to sign in with them).

They process data on our behalf or as independent controllers under their own terms. Where data could be transferred outside the European Economic Area, this only happens with appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising. There is no tracking across other apps or websites.

## How long we keep data

- Account and profile data: until you delete your account.
- Morning results in Buddies: until you delete your account or leave all clubs and ask us to remove them.
- Morning photos: 24 hours, then deleted automatically.
- Reports about content or users: up to 12 months, to handle repeat abuse.
- Anonymous statistics: kept by TelemetryDeck in aggregated form.
- Data on your iPhone: until you delete it or the app.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, receive it in a portable format, object to processing based on legitimate interest, restrict processing, and withdraw consent at any time.

- **Delete your account** directly in the app: Settings › your profile › Delete account. This deletes your account, profile, clubs you own (or hands them to another member), memberships, morning results, reactions and photos.
- **Stop statistics** in Settings › Privacy.
- For everything else, email {{privacyEmail}}. We answer within one month.

You can also file a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données), www.dataprotectionauthority.be, or with the authority in your own EU country.
