# Health & Safety

Effective 15 November 2026

Gloop is made to get you out of bed. Please read this once, so you use it safely.

## Not a medical device

Gloop is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, monitor or treat sleep or any health condition, and Gloop's messages are not medical advice. If you struggle with sleep, often feel exhausted, or think you may have a sleep disorder, talk to a doctor.

## Don't rely on it alone for critical wake-ups

Gloop uses the iPhone's alarm system, but it can only ring if:

- your iPhone is switched on, charged (or charging) and has enough storage;
- Gloop is allowed to use Alarms (check Settings › Permissions in the app);
- the alarm is switched on and not paused;
- iOS and your hardware work normally.

If missing a wake-up could have serious consequences (a flight, an exam, medication, caring for someone, a work shift), **use a second, independent alarm as well**.

Force-quitting the app stops the sound that plays during your mission, but the alarm still rings again after your re-ring time. The fail-safe stops the alarm after a set number of re-rings so you never get stuck.

## Missions: move carefully

You will usually be half-asleep when you do a mission.

- **Get up slowly.** Watch out for stairs, dark rooms, pets, cables and wet bathroom floors. Turn on a light first.
- **Shake mission:** hold your phone firmly, keep a safe distance from people and objects, and don't shake it near your face.
- **Scan a code / Scan your item:** put the code or item somewhere easy and safe to reach. Not on a high shelf, a staircase or anywhere you could fall.
- **Math mission:** take your time. Getting it wrong just gives you a new problem.
- Don't do missions while driving, cycling or doing anything that needs your full attention.

## Sound and hearing

Alarm sounds can be loud. Set a volume that wakes you without hurting your ears, especially with headphones or earbuds. If loud or sudden sounds are a health risk for you (for example some heart conditions, PTSD, or sensitivity to noise), choose a gentle sound such as Gentle Rise, or don't use a repeating alarm.

## Sleep

Gloop helps you get up, but it can't replace enough sleep. A regular bedtime, about 7 to 9 hours of sleep for most adults, and less screen time before bed make mornings easier. Gloop's evening reminder can help.

## Photos and privacy

When you share a morning photo in Buddies, make sure only you are in it, and that it shows nothing you'd rather keep private (other people, documents, your address).
