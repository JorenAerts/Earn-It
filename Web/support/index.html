Frequently asked

The alarm didn't ring

Check Settings › Permissions in Gloop: Alarms must be allowed. Gloop uses Apple's alarm system, so it rings in silent mode and Focus too.

How do I stop the alarm?

Press Stop, and Gloop opens your mission. The alarm keeps ringing (and comes back a few minutes later) until the mission is done. A fail-safe stops it after the number of rings you set.

The camera mission can't find my item

Use good light, show the item from the same side as during setup, or tap "Can't find it?" after 2 minutes for a backup mission.

How do I cancel Gloop Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Gloop. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

I bought Pro but don't see it

Gloop › Settings › Gloop Pro › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I delete my account and data?

Gloop › Settings › your profile › Delete account. This removes your account, profile, club memberships, morning results and photos. Alarms and scans only live on your iPhone.

Report content or a user

Long-press the photo or person in Buddies and choose Report, or email us. We review reports within 24 hours.
